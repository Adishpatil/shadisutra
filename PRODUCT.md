# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People planning weddings, birthdays, and functions, plus wedding guests who use a QR code to access the schedule.

## Product Purpose

The website helps people send an event-planning enquiry and lets wedding guests open the event schedule from a QR link.

## Positioning

Not confirmed. Do not invent a differentiator or make new marketing claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors enquire about an event. Wedding guests use a QR code to view its schedule.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing site supports event enquiries and schedule links.
- Contact details come from `src/config/site.ts`.
- Reuse existing rendered wording exactly. Do not add marketing copy.
- Only short, plain functional labels and necessary error or empty-state messages may be added.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Shadi Sutra.
- Keep the confirmed palette: `--bg #FDFDFF`, `--ink #16120E`, `--brand #F42902`, `--brand-strong #D92300`, `--brand-soft #FFE9E4`, and `--lime #C6F51C`.
- Decorative line art uses one consistent single-weight stroke style, without fills, shading, or gradients.
- Do not use script or handwritten fonts, emoji, sparkle icons, icon-in-circle cards, glassmorphism, fake stats, or repeated photos.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing event-planning and QR schedule flows in the repository.
- Existing site copy and image assets. Do not fabricate testimonials, statistics, awards, pricing, or other claims.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the enquiry and QR schedule journeys.
- Keep all claims grounded in existing rendered wording.
- Keep controls and schedule content accessible by keyboard and assistive technology.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for text and preserve accessible keyboard and screen-reader interaction.
