## 8. Photo alt text guide
Alt text describes what is visible, in under about 125 characters, with no "image of".
- Good: "Marigold haldi stage with yellow drapes and brass bowls of petals"
- Good: "Round banquet tables with floral centerpieces under warm lighting"
- Bad: "wedding1" or "photo"

Name photo files by category prefix before running the image script:
`hero-*`, `about-*`, `weddings-*`, `birthdays-*`, `functions-*`
